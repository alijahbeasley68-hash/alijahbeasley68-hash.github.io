🎯 What Is This?

adhd-study-coach is a smart study assistant that works directly with Claude Code. It was designed specifically for people with ADHD who find it hard to stay focused during study sessions. Instead of dumping huge amounts of information on you, this tool breaks everything down into small, manageable lessons that your brain can actually handle.

Think of it like having a personal tutor who understands how your mind works. It uses three powerful techniques:

  • Spaced Repetition - Reviews material at the perfect time so you remember it long-term
  • Small-Chunk Teaching - Learn in short bursts that don't overwhelm you
  • Feynman Reflection - Explain concepts in simple terms to really lock them in

🔧 How It Works

This tool lives right inside Claude Code, which is a program you can use to talk to AI. When you use adhd-study-coach, it acts like a study coach that:

  • Tracks what topics you're learning
  • Schedules review times for you automatically
  • Asks you simple questions to check your understanding
  • Helps you explain concepts in your own words
  • Celebrates your wins to keep you motivated

Whether you're studying for school, learning a new skill, or preparing for certifications, this tool keeps you on track without feeling like a chore.
